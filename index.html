Need a hand?

Email alex@omniabi.com with what happened and your iPhone model. Please leave passwords and sign-in links out of your message.

Confirming your account

Open the latest confirmation email on the same iPhone where you created your account. If the link has expired, use “Resend confirmation email” in the app.

Resetting your password

Enter your email on the sign-in screen and tap “Forgot password?”. Open the latest email on that iPhone and choose a new password of at least 10 characters.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Account → Delete account. Enter your current password and type DELETE to confirm. This permanently removes your online account and its games, venues, bookmarks and seat requests. The local preview is stored separately on your iPhone.
