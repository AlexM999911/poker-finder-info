Poker Finder beta privacy notice

Who runs Poker Finder

Poker Finder is operated by Omniabi Ltd. For privacy requests or support, contact alex@omniabi.com. This notice covers the iPhone beta and its shared backend. Updated 13 September 2026.

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address, player name, account identifier and authentication information. Supabase processes your password for authentication. We also store games you publish, photos and videos you choose to upload, venue details and contact information you enter, saved games, seat requests and host decisions. Service providers process IP addresses and technical logs to deliver and protect the service. If you use Apple or Google, that provider authenticates you and supplies an account identifier and email address, which may be an Apple relay address. We never receive your Apple or Google password. We collect your date of birth, country of residence and a timestamped legal-age declaration before you use shared games. We store notification preferences, in-app game updates and read status. If you enable iPhone notifications, we register an app-specific Apple push token linked to your signed-in session.

How your information is used

We use account and game information to provide the service you request: signing in, publishing and finding games, and managing seat requests. This processing is necessary to provide our service. We use technical information for our legitimate interests in security, preventing misuse and fixing faults. Required account details are necessary to use shared games; the local preview can be used without an account.

What other players can see

Signed-in players can see publicly listed games, including the host name, area, stakes, time, notes, photos and video. A host can see the names and requests of players requesting their games. Players can see the status of their own requests. Saved games and venue manager contact details are visible only to their owner through the app. Listed venue profiles include facilities, house rules, transport information and host-supplied legal details. Invitation-only venue profiles, games and media are accessible to their host and accounts that redeem an invitation code. Anyone given the code can join; share codes privately. Hosts can see how requesting players say they were introduced. Previously issued media links may remain usable until their short expiry. Do not put private addresses or sensitive information in public game notes, and provide another person’s venue contact details only with their permission. Your date of birth, country and age declaration are not shared with hosts or other players. They are accessible only to your account and authorised service operators.

Venue declarations and posting credits

We store the version and time of host and player declarations, copies of the relevant venue details or rules, invitation memberships and the introduction choice made with a seat request. These records support venue access, rules acknowledgement and dispute handling. We also store a private balance and transaction history for free venue posting credits. Deleting a venue removes its related declarations and memberships; deleting an account removes its own declaration and credit records. Credits have no cash value.

Our service providers

Supabase hosts the database, uploaded media and authentication service; the primary database is in London. Resend sends signup and password-reset emails using Omniabi’s account. These providers receive the information needed to perform these tasks, and authorised operators can access records when necessary for support and security. Their infrastructure and subprocessors may process information outside the UK. Their privacy and data-processing terms describe the applicable transfer safeguards: https://supabase.com/privacy and https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy. Apple Push Notification service delivers optional iPhone alerts. Push payloads contain a generic message, not player names, game details or your date of birth.

Retention and account deletion

Account records and shared app content are kept until you delete them or your account. Use Account → Delete account to permanently remove your account, games and their uploaded media, venues, bookmarks and seat requests from the live service. Requests associated with games you hosted are also removed. Other people may already have copied information you shared. Provider email-delivery and security logs expire under the providers’ retention settings and are not erased by the in-app deletion action. Limited records needed to resolve a support request, investigate abuse or meet legal obligations may be retained for those purposes. Inbox updates are retained for up to 90 days. Inactive push registrations expire after 30 days; delivery records are removed seven days after completion. Signing out unregisters the device, and server delivery checks that its linked session is still active. Turning off push removes the account’s registered devices. Game updates already received by other players may remain in their inboxes until expiry.

Data stored on your iPhone

Login credentials for the session are kept in the iPhone Keychain. Signing out or deleting your account clears the app’s current login session and online data in memory. The separate local preview stores games, venues and saved choices on the device and never uploads them automatically. Local preview entries are separate from your online account; remove them in the preview or remove the app to clear its local files.

Your choices and rights

You can request access to your personal information, correction, deletion, restriction or a portable copy by contacting alex@omniabi.com. Where processing relies on legitimate interests, you can object. We may need to verify your identity before acting. You can raise a privacy concern with us or complain to the UK Information Commissioner at https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/. Rights depend on the circumstances and applicable law.

Maps and directions

Map buttons open Apple Maps on Apple devices or Google Maps on Android (in its app or browser), and send the selected area, public venue name or address you enter to the corresponding provider. An address entered for directions is held only on the current screen and is not saved to your account or sent to Poker Finder’s backend. The map provider handles your starting point, location permission and route under its own privacy terms. Poker Finder does not request or read GPS location.

Tracking and changes

This beta does not include advertising, advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking or third-party product-analytics SDKs. It does not request GPS location, contacts or microphone access. Hosts can choose photos and a video from their library to upload; only selected files are uploaded. Areas in game listings are entered manually. Authentication emails are service messages, not a marketing subscription. We will update this notice when the app’s data practices change.

This information website

GitHub Pages hosts this privacy and support website and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages do not use advertising or analytics scripts.
